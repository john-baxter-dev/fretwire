# fretwire 0.4.0

**Your HX Edit backups open on Linux.** An `.hxb` device backup converts into presets the pedal
accepts — blocks, parameters, snapshots, footswitches and controller assignments — and a verified
restore puts them back on the hardware. Alongside that: **custom footswitch labels and ring
colours** (the 0.3.0 known-limit, closed), the Globals panel grown to the pedal's own six menus,
the **POD Go** and **HX Effects** recognised, and the CLI built for **arm64**.

66 commits since [`v0.3.0`](https://github.com/john-baxter-dev/fretwire/releases/tag/v0.3.0).

---

## Backup import

HX Edit's backups are the format your presets already live in. fretwire now reads them:

- **Inspect** — `fretwire show-backup <file.hxb>` lists a backup's setlists and presets, offline.
- **Convert** — `fretwire hxb-convert <file.hxb> <out.json>` turns every preset into the device's
  own wire format. Offline, and checkable: the conversion is verified against presets held in
  *both* forms — a contributor's Floor backup and wire dumps of the same slots off the same unit —
  and the reference backup converts **363 of 363** non-empty presets.
- **Restore** — `fretwire restore <out.json> <slot>` writes one preset into a setlist slot.
  Verified end-to-end on hardware: export, restore, and the flash read-back is byte-identical.

What carries: every block type (amps with their cabs, both cab generations, dual cabs, IRs by
their user-slot reference, split/mixer topology, the looper, even the synths), all parameter
values, snapshot names and per-snapshot state, the footswitch layout with custom labels and
colours, and the full controller-assignment tables — expression pedals, footswitches and
snapshots, byte-equal to what the device itself writes.

What doesn't carry is **said, never guessed**: the conversion reports what it skipped and why
(MIDI-sourced assignments and a couple of corners listed under Known limits), and refuses a
preset it cannot represent rather than producing a plausible-looking wrong one.

> Restore is a **flash write** — it overwrites the target slot, with a confirmation naming what is
> about to be lost. Export first; the same tooling makes that easy (`export-setlist`).

Finding the restore path also found a bug worth having on the record: an op-21 preset write sent
straight after a `goto` stalls the transfer deterministically. Reading the preset first re-opens
the edit buffer and the write proceeds; `restore` does this itself now.

## Custom footswitch labels and ring colours

0.3.0's known limits said: *"Footswitch ring colours and labels can be read, not written... setting
a custom one needs ops we have not decoded, and probing them blind wedged a pedal."* Closed — and
without those ops. The label and colour turn out to live in the preset document itself, so they
ride the same whole-preset write the restore work proved out, and the dangerous opcodes stay
untouched.

- In the **GUI**: a ✎ next to the footswitch picker on any block that's on a switch opens a label
  field and the colour palette. Changes land in the edit buffer, undoable like any other edit, and
  the pedal's ring and scribble strip repaint immediately.
- From the **CLI**: `fretwire switch-label <fs> [text]` and `switch-color <fs> [1-11]` — no
  argument clears.
- The **palette is the device's own**, mapped end to end: the twelve values are the
  `footswitchLED` list in Line 6's shipped data (Auto, White, Red, Dark Orange, Light Orange,
  Yellow, Green, Turquoise, Blue, Violet, Pink, Off), and every one was confirmed against a real
  ring — including Off, which is exactly that.
- `hxb-convert` carries `@fs_customlabel` and `@fs_customcolor`, so backups with customised
  switches restore with them intact.

## Global settings, in the pedal's own menus

The Globals panel now mirrors **all six of the pedal's menu sections** — Ins/Outs, Preferences,
Footswitches, EXP Pedals, MIDI/Tempo, Displays — in the pedal's own order, with newly named
settings among them (Snapshot CC Send, Return Type, Auto In-Z's values). The sections, and the
XL rows that came with them, were contributed and verified on hardware by **Robert Tsai**.

Also fixed here: the HX Stomp XL reports its preset range as `000`-`128`; that last number is the
XL's own firmware bug, and the panel now shows the range the pedal actually has.

## Devices

Two new entries, and real captures for a third:

| Device | PID | Status |
|---|---|---|
| HX Stomp | `0x4246` | **verified** — developed against one |
| Helix Floor | `0x4248` | **verified** — ~70 logged sessions with a remote tester |
| Helix LT | `0x424a` | **reads verified, edits untested** |
| HX Stomp XL | `0x4253` | **reported working** — and now surveyed from its own preset streams |
| HX Effects | `0x4245` | **reported working** — recognised, no session logged yet |
| POD Go | `0x4247` | **reads and writes reconciled** — an owner drives one from the editor |

- The **POD Go** (issue #15) speaks the identical protocol with entirely different reference data:
  its own symbol table, so it needs **POD Go Edit's** data imported rather than HX Edit's (the
  importer keeps both side by side). Reads and writes both reproduce its captured bytes exactly —
  see [`docs/pod-go.md`](https://github.com/john-baxter-dev/fretwire/blob/v0.4.0/docs/pod-go.md).
  Its fixed-chain add/move/delete is not mapped yet.
- The **HX Effects** (issue #10) is recognised from an owner's `lsusb` line — **@kolbiman** — and
  reported working; that report is the whole of what we hold, so nothing about its geometry is
  assumed.
- The **HX Stomp XL** stopped being extrapolated: two preset streams off a real one (Robert Tsai,
  issue #13) settle one DSP, four snapshots, eight footswitches and a 13-entry controller table.
  The fixes that followed matter on every device: expression-pedal bypasses now *show* (they used
  to render as unassigned), the footswitch picker is sized by the preset instead of capped at
  five, and MIDI/Snapshots source ordinals move with the switch count instead of being hard-coded.

## Headless and arm64

- The udev rule now grants access by **group** (`plugdev`) as well as by seat, so the pedal works
  over **SSH and from services** — the seat-based grant only ever covered someone logged into the
  console. `fretwire install-udev` creates the group; join it and replug.
- The release ships a **native arm64 CLI** (`fretwire-cli-aarch64-linux-musl.tar.gz`) alongside
  x86-64 — for the Pi-class machines a headless pedal tends to be plugged into.

## Fixes

- **A cab parameter pushed from the pedal could land on the amp.** Status pushes name the paired
  model in key 26; it was dropped, so turning a cab's knob on the pedal moved the amp's slider of
  the same index in the GUI. Writes were always correct; only the mirror was wrong.
- **`clear-preset`** empties the loaded preset properly: blocks, controller assignments and
  snapshot names, matching what the pedal means by an empty preset.
- The **amp+cab pairing survey** that unblocked the backup import also produced ten new tracked
  fixtures and the complete block-class table in
  [`docs/preset-format.md`](https://github.com/john-baxter-dev/fretwire/blob/v0.4.0/docs/preset-format.md)
  — measured on the pedal, not inferred.

## Also

- The **cab's mic placement is drawn** — position, distance and angle as a diagram rather than
  three numbers.
- The window opens wide enough to show a whole chain.
- The GUI warns when its embedded frontend is older than the UI sources it sits next to — a
  dev-build foot-gun, defused.

## Known limits

- **Backup conversion has three named gaps**: MIDI-sourced controller assignments (their wire row
  carries a CC field no sample shows), the Floor's `A/B` split topology, and the four I/O nodes'
  parameter blocks. Each is skipped *and reported* per preset; nothing is silently dropped.
- **Grid and routing planning is still DSP-0 only.** Reading and per-block edits are DSP-agnostic;
  moving and inserting blocks plans inside the first 20-slot array.
- **The settings namespace is mapped only in part** — 166 ids answer on a Stomp, 28 are named.
  `settings-diff` finds a new one in about thirty seconds if you want to help.
- **IR reorder is not implemented.** Delete and re-upload expresses it.
- **No firmware, flash or DFU traffic**, by design and permanently. See
  [`docs/safety.md`](https://github.com/john-baxter-dev/fretwire/blob/v0.4.0/docs/safety.md).

## Installing

Grab the `.deb`, `.rpm` or `.AppImage` for the GUI, or a static CLI tarball —
`fretwire-cli-x86_64-linux-musl.tar.gz` or `fretwire-cli-aarch64-linux-musl.tar.gz` — that runs on
any distro. The deb and rpm install the udev rule and the CLI alongside the app; for the AppImage
or the tarballs, install `packaging/70-hxstomp.rules` yourself and replug the pedal.

On first run the app imports the model and preset reference data **from your own HX Edit
installation** (POD Go owners: from POD Go Edit). Nothing proprietary is shipped in these
packages. Skipping the import is allowed — the editor works without it, with numeric names
instead.
